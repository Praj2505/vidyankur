Why It Works

Built for the way you already teach.

You don't need a curriculum change. You need a single book that fills one activity slot per week — and keeps children asking for more.

🎨

Art & Craft Sessions

Replace blank-sheet coloring with pages that mean something. Children color Shivaji's coronation, Chetak leaping across the river, a Maratha fort on a hilltop.

🎤

Storytelling & Recitation

Every book has a four-stanza rhyme designed to be memorized in one sitting. Perfect for weekly recitation or show-and-tell with parents.

🧩

Activity & Puzzle Time

Connect-the-dots, mazes, find-the-difference — built into every book. Children finish the session having learned something without realizing they studied.

Ready to Use

A sample 30-minute session.

Here's how one book can power a single class. No prep, no printouts, no extra material.

5 min

The Story

Read aloud the hero's story. One page, age-appropriate, written for young listeners.

5 min

The Rhyme

Read the four-stanza rhyme together. By the second week, children recite it from memory.

15 min

Coloring Time

Children color one of two full-page illustrations. They take the book home — parents see the output.

5 min

Activity Page

A quick maze, connect-the-dots, or spot-the-difference wraps up the session.

Total: 30 minutes · One book · Zero preparation
