Quick Answers

Frequently asked.

Where can I buy Vidyankur books?

All our books are available on Amazon India. We're also working on direct shipping — drop us a message if you'd like to be notified.

Can my school adopt Vidyankur?

Yes. We plan to work with primary schools (Classes 1–3 primarily) to include our annual workbook in their book list. The Program is Launching soon. Learn about the School Program →

Do you ship outside India?

Currently we ship within India only. For international orders, please email us — we may be able to arrange something special.

Are bulk discounts available?

Yes. We offer significant discounts for schools, tuition centers, and bulk orders above 30 copies. See pricing →

Can I request a sample copy?

Schools and institutional buyers can request a free sample. Individual parents can preview books on Amazon or through our "Peek Inside" feature on the Books page.

I want to partner / collaborate. Who do I contact?

For partnerships, distribution, illustration collaborations, or media inquiries — please email vidyankurpublication@gmail.com with the subject line "Partnership".
