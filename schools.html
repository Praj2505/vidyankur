The Problem

Classes 1–3 have no dedicated heritage content.

EVS

covers nature, weather, and the environment.

Moral Science

covers values — honesty, sharing, kindness.

English / Hindi

cover language and reading.

?

No subject teaches young children about Shivaji, Rani Lakshmibai, or Maharana Pratap.

NEP 2020 mandates the integration of Indian Knowledge Systems from the foundational stage. Schools need content. Vidyankur is the content.

The Solution

Inside the Workbook

What every child gets.

Each of the 12 monthly sections follows the same dependable structure — so teachers know what to expect.

Page 1

The Story

A short, age-appropriate retelling of the hero's life and achievement.

Page 2

The Rhyme

A four-stanza rhyme designed to be memorized and recited.

Pages 3–4

Coloring Spreads

Two full-page illustrations — the hero, and a key scene from their life.

Page 5

The Activity

Connect-the-dots, mazes, find-the-difference — playful, on-theme.

Page 6

I Learned

A fill-in-the-blank reflection page. Children write what they remember.

Page 7

The Certificate

A tear-out completion certificate for every hero. Children love these.
