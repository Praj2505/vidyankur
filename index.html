Our Mission

Planting the seeds of Indian heritage in young minds.

A child who colors Shivaji's portrait remembers him. A child who recites a rhyme about Maharana Pratap internalizes courage. Vidyankur turns India's greatest stories into the books your child will love most.

Rhyming Stories

Simple rhymes that turn complex history into songs a 4-year-old can recite. Each verse carefully crafted to be memorable and true.

Coloring Pages

Hand-illustrated portraits of forts, kings, horses, and battles — designed to be colored, framed, and remembered.

Real Heroes

No fictional characters. No watered-down history. The actual men and women who shaped India — told with dignity and joy.
