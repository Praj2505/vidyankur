Vidyankur Young Historians

A nationwide stage
for young historians.

Three competitions. One academic year. Open to every Vidyankur partner school. Designed to celebrate children who fall in love with India's heroes.

The Programs

Three ways to compete.

Children participate at their school level. Winners advance to the regional and national rounds.

I

Heritage Drawing Competition

"Color your hero."

Children submit a colored or hand-drawn portrait of any Indian hero from the Vidyankur library. Judged on color sense, expression, and storytelling.

  • Open to: Classes 1–5
  • Format: A4 hand-drawn or colored from Vidyankur book
  • Held: October–November
II

Heritage Quiz

"What do you remember?"

A friendly, age-appropriate quiz on Indian heroes — based entirely on Vidyankur content. School-level rounds lead to a final on Republic Day.

  • Open to: Classes 3–7
  • Format: Team of 3 per school
  • Held: January
III

Storytelling Competition

"Tell us a story."

Children retell the story of an Indian hero in their own words — in English, Hindi, or any Indian language. Judged on confidence, clarity, and creativity.

  • Open to: Classes 1–5
  • Format: 3-minute oral telling, any language
  • Held: February–March
